# Third-party notices

`dist/vis-capture-replay.iife.js`, the script-tag build of the replay entry, bundles the
recorder of [rrweb](https://github.com/rrweb-io/rrweb) (`@rrweb/record`) and the code its build
bundles. Their licence notices follow. The other files of the package bundle no third-party code;
`dist/replay.js` imports `@rrweb/record` and `@rrweb/types` from npm instead.
